Recording cash and banking transactions

Completing double entry for items
recorded in the three-column cash book

When completing double entries for
items entered in the cash book, posting of
figures from the discount columns of the
cash book is done as follows:

The individual amounts of the discount
column on the debit side represent
discount allowed to different debtors.

These are posted to the sales ledger

(individual

accounts

of

customers/

debtors), by crediting such accounts.

This is a posting to subsidiary ledger. The
total amount of this column is debited to
discount allowed expense account and
credited to the trade receivables account
in the general ledger.

The individual amounts of the discount
column on the credit side represent
discount

received

from

different

creditors. These are posted to the
purchase ledger (individual accounts of
suppliers/creditors). These accounts in
the subsidiary ledger are debited with the
discounts. For general ledger, completing
the double entry involves posting the
total amount of this column to the debit
side of trade payables account. The
corresponding entry will be a credit entry
to the discount received account.
