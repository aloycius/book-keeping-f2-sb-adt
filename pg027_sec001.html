Task 1.1

You have just completed your lessons on double-column cash book and started a new discussion on three-column cash book. Nathan, your classmate has just read a line from the introduction that when one has more than one bank account, there could be more than two or three-columns in the cash book. He is now challenged what will happen to a business with six bank accounts. His idea is that there would be too many columns if the cash book will have a column for each of the bank accounts. He suggests that one bank column can be used to record all the bank account transactions. He is also of the opinion that there must be a maximum number of columns in the cash book and businesses should not exceed such maximum. Write a summary of your explanation concerning:

(a) The maximum number of columns in the cash book.

(b) Recording of transactions with different banks in the same column of the cash book.
