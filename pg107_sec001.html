Student’s Book Form Two

Reconciling bank account with cash book balances

Key term Definition Related inconsistency
Direct orders Regular automatic payments received through the bank account Money received directly into the bank account but missing in the cash book
Standing orders Regular automatic payments authorised from the bank account Payments are deducted automatically by the bank but not yet recorded in the cash book
Dishonoured cheques Cheques rejected by the bank due to insufficient funds errors or any other reasons A cheque was recorded as received or issued in the cash book but later rejected by the bank
Errors in the cash book Mistakes in recording transactions in the cash book Amounts are recorded incorrectly, leading to discrepancies
Errors in the bank statement Mistakes made by the bank when processing transactions Incorrect transactions recorded by the bank, leading to inconsistencies
Credit transfers Payments received directly into the bank account The business may not be aware of these transfers until checking the bank statement
Overdraft When withdrawals exceed the bank balance The cash book may not reflect the overdraft if not recorded properly

Activity 3.3

Some companies experience discrepancies between their cash book and bank statement balances. Visit any nearby company and consult the accountant to explore the reasons behind the differences. Afterwards, write a summary of the findings based on your discussions.
