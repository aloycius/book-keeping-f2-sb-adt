Student’s Book Form Two

When a cheque is dishonoured, it would not be debited in the bank statement. After dealing with the problem that led to the dishonour, the business will have to cancel the cheque and issue a fresh one. The entry for cancelling the cheque is to debit the cash book when adjusting it.

Example 3.3 demonstrates on how to prepare the bank reconciliation statement when there are errors and dishonoured cheques.
