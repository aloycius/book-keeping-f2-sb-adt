11. A family starts the month with cash balance of TZS 600,000. They expect to have cash inflow from a salary income of TZS 2,500,000 and TZS 300,000 from a small business. Their estimated expenditures include rent TZS 900,000, food

TZS 600,000, transport TZS 200,000, and entertainment TZS 150,000. Prepare
a cash budget of the family for that month and determine the estimated closing
cash balance.

12. A retail shop expected to begin a month with an opening cash balance of TZS

800,000. The expected monthly cash inflows include sales revenue of TZS 5,000,000

and a planned loan of TZS 1,200,000. The estimated expenditures for that month

include rent of TZS 1,500,000, stock purchases of TZS 2,500,000, salaries of TZS

1,200,000, and utilities of TZS 300,000. Prepare a cash budget of the retail shop
for that month and determine the estimated closing cash balance.

13. Jane just started a month with an opening cash balance of TZS 50,000. She expects to receive pocket money of TZS 300,000 and a scholarship allowance of TZS 200,000. Her monthly expenditures include books TZS 100,000, meals

TZS 250,000, transport TZS 80,000, and entertainment TZS 50,000. Prepare a
cash budget for Jane and determine the estimated closing cash balance at the end
of month.

14. A farmer expected to start the season with cash balance of TZS 1,500,000. Also,
he expected to have cash inflows from crop sales TZS 6,500,000 and a grant TZS

2,000,000. The farmer expected to incur expenditure on seeds TZS 2,000,000,
fertilisers TZS 3,000,000, labour TZS 1,800,000, and transport TZS 1,200,000.

Prepare a cash budget and determine the closing cash balance.

15. A manufacturing company expected to commence a financial year with an opening cash balance of TZS 5,000,000. The company also expected to have cash inflows from sales of TZS 20,000,000 and from investment activities of TZS 2,500,000.

Annual estimated expenditures include acquisition of machinery TZS 10,000,000,

wages TZS 4,000,000, rent TZS 3,000,000, and electricity TZS 2,500,000. Prepare

a cash budget for the financial year and determine the closing cash balance of
the company.
