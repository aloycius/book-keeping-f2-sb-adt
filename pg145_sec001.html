Making adjustments of cash budget

When a budget shows a cash deficit,
a person or business needs to make
adjustments to improve their financial
stability. The following are some of the
ways to balance the budget:

Increasing revenue sources:
One way to
improve cash flow is to find additional
sources of revenue. For example, a tied
shop owner can introduce mobile money
services or start selling new products,
such as phone accessories to attract more
customers. Individuals and businesses
can reduce financial pressure when they
have multiple revenue sources.This
ensures they have enough money to cover
their needs.

Reducing unnecessary expenses: Cutting

down unnecessary costs can help to

prevent running out of money. For
example, a clothing store owner can
avoid overbuying stock. A restaurant
owner can save on electricity, turning off
appliances when they are not in use and
using energy-efficient equipment. This
can ensure businesses have more money
available for important expenditures.

Negotiating better deals with suppliers:

Businesses can improve their cash flow by
getting better prices or supplier payment
terms. For example, a poultry farmer can
negotiate to buy chicken feed in bulk at
a discounted price rather than making
small expensive purchases.

Prioritising essential expenses: The business can avoid financial problems and meet basic needs by prioritising the

most important costs. For example, a
small business owner should pay for rent,
employee wages, and essential supplies
before spending money on unnecessary
upgrades. The business can avoid financial
problem
s and meet basic needs by

prioritising the most important costs.

Saving for unforeseen expenses: Setting

aside part of cash inflows for emergence
expenses can help to avoid financial
problems in the future. For example, a
tailor can save a small amount each month
for repairing the sewing machine once
breaks down. A shop owner can create a
reserve fund to cover unexpected costs
like equipment breakdowns or inventory
shortages.
This ensures that financial

emergencies do not disrupt business
operations.
