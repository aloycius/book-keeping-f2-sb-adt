Recording cash and banking transactions

Introduction

Keeping records of cash and banking transactions is essential for everyone. To manage your pocket money properly, you must keep some records of your transactions. Businesses have systems to record receipts and payments of cash and banking transactions that form part of their day-to-day operations. In this chapter, you will learn the meaning of cash and banking transactions, the types of cash book, and preparation of cash books (single-column, two-column, three-column, and the petty cash books). The competencies developed will enable you to record cash and banking transactions in different types of cash books appropriately.

Meaning of cash and banking transactions

Cash transactions is a form of payment/ receipt in which cash money is exchanged for goods or services during the time of purchase, sale or any other payments/ receipts. This transaction uses physical money such as banknotes and coins. Examples of cash payments are; Juma buys motor vehicles and machinery for his business and then immediately pays the seller a total amount TZS 14,000,000, in cash, Juliet pays cash TZS 300,000 for light and heating charges to Jm Co. Ltd. Another example on cash receipt, a company borrows money from the bank

and received it in cash. Cash transactions involve the inflow of money (cash receipts) and outflow of money (cash payments). Cash receipts refer to money received, for example, the sources of cash receipts for a business may include cash sales, customer payments, or refunds. On the other hand, cash payments represent money spent including expenses, supplier payments, and debt settlements.
