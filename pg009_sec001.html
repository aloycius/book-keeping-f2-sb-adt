Recording cash and banking transactions

Introduction

Keeping records of cash and banking transactions is essential for everyone.

To manage your pocket money properly, you must keep some records of your
transactions. Businesses have systems to record receipts and payments of cash and
banking transactions that form part of their day-to-day operations. In this chapter,
you will learn the meaning of cash and banking transactions, the types of cash
book, and preparation of cash books (single-column, two-column, three-column,
and the petty cash books). The competencies developed will enable you to record
cash and banking transactions in different types of cash books appropriately.
