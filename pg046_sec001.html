Student’s Book Form Two

Note: Cash is an asset that needs to be reported in the financial statements, specifically, the statement of financial position. The balance of cash for use in the financial statements will be the sum of balances from all the cash books including the petty cash book. It will also include balances in all the mobile money accounts that the business has at the date of the financial statements.

Chapter summary 1. A cash book is used for recording the flow of cash into and out of the business. It records cash receipts and cash payments for a business. 2. Development in modern payment systems and controls has brought a necessity to have different types of cash books, namely; Single-column cash book, Two- column cash book, Three-column cash book and Petty cash book. 3. Cash receipts are recorded on the debit side while cash payments are recorded on the credit side of the single, two, and three-column cash books. 4. Two-column cash books enable the business to monitor cash transactions through reviewing all the cash movements in the same place. Also it helps the business manager to see the cash and bank balance at any point in time and making appropriate decisions. 5. Any business venture needs to prepare a petty cash book as it helps to record small cash payments or petty cash payments. 6. When the business uses accounting software packages in its operations, it can prepare its cash books easily.
