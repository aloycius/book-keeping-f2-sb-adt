Preparing bank reconciliation
statement using unadjusted cash book
balance

It is possible to prepare a bank
reconciliation statement without first
adjusting the cash book balance. This
is because the reconciling and adjusting
items, as well as errors cause the cash book
balance differ from the bank statement
balance. However, the cash book will still
need to be adjusted. The adjustment could
be done monthly or in the final month of
the accounting period. This is necessary
for determining the bank balance to be
reported in the financial statements.

When preparing bank reconciliation
statement using unadjusted cash book

balance, all items causing the difference

between the cash book balance and bank

statement balance are treated as reconciling items. It is important to identify the effect

of each on the relationship between the
unadjusted cash book balance and the
bank statement balance. Preparation of
a bank reconciliation statement starting
with either the unadjusted cash book
balance or the bank statement balance can
be done.
