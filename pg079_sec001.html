Student’s Book Form Two

Correction of Book-keeping errors and the Trial balance

Solution

The first step is to determine the balance of the suspense account. This can be done by rearranging the uncorrected balances in the trial balance. The rearrangement of the trial balance is done by making sure that the accounts with debit balances are entered in the debit column, and those with credit balances are entered in the credit column. It is therefore necessary to be conversant with the normal balances for each account class. The rearranged accounts have been highlighted in purple colour. Note that upon rearranging the balances in the trial balance, the credit column total is TZS 466,908,000, which is TZS 841,750 higher than the TZS 466,066,250 debit column total. This means that a suspense account balance of TZS 841,750 must be inserted on the debit column. The trial balance, before correcting the errors (i) – (v) will be as follows:
