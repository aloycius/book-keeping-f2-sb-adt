Student’s Book Form Two

Correction of Book-keeping errors and the Trial balance

Example 2.1

A credit purchase of goods worth TZS 50,000 from Daudi Salum was omitted from the books. To correct this error, the missing double entry is made to record the purchases and the trade payables account in the books. The journal entry for the correction is:

Journal Entry

Date Details Dr (TZS) Cr (TZS)
Purchases 50,000
Trade payables 50,000
Being error of omission now corrected

Correcting an error of commission In this case, one of the entries has been made in a wrong account. The correct double entry should be made such that: (i) It cancels or offsets the entry made in the wrong account; and (ii) It records the transaction in the appropriate account. Example 2.2 illustrates this.

Example 2.2

A credit sale of goods for TZS 100,000 to Mrema is entered in the account of Mremi. This sale was supposed to be debited in Mrema’s account. It was instead debited to Mremi’s account. To correct this error, a double entry is made such that the record is cancelled from the personal account of Mremi and entered to Mrema`s personal account.

Journal Entry

Date Details Dr (TZS) Cr (TZS)
Mrema 100,000
Mremi 100,000
Being an error of commission now corrected
