Reconciling bank account with cash book balances

Reconciliation without reversing the reconciling items The same structure of the bank reconciliation statement that starts with normal balances can be maintained while reconciling in an overdraft situation. The key point is to recognise the fact that an overdraft is, arithmetically, a negative balance. This means that the overdraft (as per bank statement or as per cash book) can be presented as a negative figure, and the bank reconciliation statement can proceed as usual. It is also possible either to start with the cash book balance or the bank statement balance.

Starting with overdraft as per cash book

When starting with the overdraft as per cash book, the bank reconciliation statement is as follows:

Bank reconciliation statement as at……

Amount
Adjusted balance (overdraft) as per cash book (xxx)
Add: Unpresented cheques/ outstanding cheques xxx
xxx
Less: Uncredited cheques/ deposits in transit/ uncleared deposits xxx
Balance (overdraft) as per bank statement (xxx)

It is important to note that it is possible to have an overdraft in the cash book and have a favourable balance in the bank statement.
