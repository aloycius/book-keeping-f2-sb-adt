Budgeting and budgetary control

Glossary

A bank is a financial institution authorised by the government to conduct banking deposits, advancing credit, withdrawals, and provide general utility services to customers

A bursar is an official in charge of funds at school, college or university

Account balance is the difference between the total of debit and credit entries (including the beginning balance) in an account at a particular date

An altered cheque is a cheque that has been intentionally changed, usually for fraudulent purposes. It may involve a change in the name of the payee, the amount of the cheques, or the date

Book-keeping is an integral part of accounting that involves recording and organising all the business transactions that have occurred in the course of the business

Cash currency notes and coins together with amounts deposited in bank current or business accounts

Cheque clearing is a process whereby funds move from one bank account to another to settle a cheque payment

Cheques register another name for the cash disbursement journal when it has a column for cheque numbers

Contra entry is an entry recorded when the debit and credit affect the same business account and result in a net zero effect on the account. These are transactions that are recorded between cash and bank account columns

Cost driver is a factor that contributes in making the total cost to change i.e either to increase or decrease. For example, if a small manufacturing company pays its production workers based on hours worked, then the total cost of labour will either increase or decrease depending with number of hours worked. In this case, labour hours is the cost drivers. Alternatively, if the company pays the workers based pieces produced, then the number of units produced will be the cost driver for total labour cost

Deficit is the excess of expenditure over revenues or collections for a period

Disbursement is the payment of cash for various expenses or obligations

Expenditure is the amount spent or about to be spent on paying for goods and services

Imprest system is a method to account for petty cash that maintains an approved balance

Overdraft is the arrangement that allows a bank customer (account holder) to withdraw more money than the account holds

Revenue is a total amount of income generated by a business

Surplus is the excess of revenues or collection over expenditure for a particular period

Taxes are compulsory payments made by individuals and businesses to the government

Vendor is a person or business that sells goods or services
