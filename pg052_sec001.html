Student’s Book Form Two

15. Ndamatile is a sole trader who maintains her petty cash book on imprest system, the cash float being TZS 1,200,000 at the beginning of each month with a maximum of TZS 200,000 per petty cash voucher. You are given the following petty cash transactions for the month of September 2023.

TZS 1/09/2023 Cheque received from chief cashier as petty cash float 1,105,000

2/09/2023 Paid carriage 52,000

3/09/2023 Bought fuel for office use 127,000

3/09/2023 Paid carriage 85,000

4/09/2023 Bought envelopes 100,000

5/09/2023 Paid window cleaning 83,000

5/09/2023 Bought diesel for office van 143,000

6/09/2023 Bought petrol 167,000

7/09/2023 Paid carriage 124,000

18/09/2023 Bought manila sheets 132,000

20/09/2023 Paid office cleaning expenses 77,000

28/09/2023 Bought soft drinks for staff 110,000

Required:

(a) Prepare a petty cash book with analysis columns for carriage, motor expenses, stationeries and sundry expenses.

(b) Enter the receipt of the amount necessary to restore the imprest at the end of the month and carry down the balance for the commencement of the following month. (c) Show how the double entry will be completed for each expenditure item.
