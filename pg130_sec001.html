Student’s Book Form Two

Starting with overdraft as per bank statement To prepare a bank reconciliation statement starting with the overdraft as per bank statement, the focus should be on the effect that the reconciling items have on the relationship between the overdraft as per bank statement and the overdraft as per cash book. Unpresented cheques and direct deposits would cause the overdraft as per bank statement to be lower than the overdraft as per cash book. On the other hand, uncredited cheques and direct debits such as standing orders and bank charges will cause the overdraft as per bank statement to be higher than the overdraft as per cash book.
