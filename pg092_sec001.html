8. When a trial balance totals on the debit column and credit column agrees, it is not possible for the trial balance to have errors. Argue against by providing reasons.

9. The trial balance of Elia shows the debit column is TZS 5,946,000 greater than the credit column. The difference was placed in the suspense account and the following errors were found:

(i) The sales journal was overcast by TZS 450,000.

(ii) Cash received from Chausiku in full settlement of her account of TZS 900,000 was posted in cash book, but no posting was made to trade receivables account.

(iii) A cheque of TZS 1,500,000 paid to creditors was correctly recorded in the cash book, but no corresponding entry was made to the trade payables account.

(iv) The total of sales journal of TZS 3,600,000 was correctly entered to the trade receivables account, and debited to sales returns account.

(v) A credit sale of goods of TZS 690,000 to Sanga was posted in sales account but corresponding entry to trade receivables account was not done.

(vi) One page total of the sales journal of TZS 840,000 was posted to the credit side of sales account in general ledger as TZS 624,000.

(vii) An amount of TZS 300,000 received as interest was credited to interest income account as TZS 30,000.

(viii) A cheque for TZS 435,000 received from Mwanaharusi was correctly entered in trade receivables account, and credited in the cash book.

(ix) One cash payment of TZS 420,000 was not included when calculating the cash book balance.

Required:

(a) Show the necessary journal entries to correct the given errors. (b) Prepare the suspense account.
