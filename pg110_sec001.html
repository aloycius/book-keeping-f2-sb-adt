Task

Asha was absent during the Book-keeping
lesson when the teacher explained how to
prepare a bank reconciliation statement
using the adjusted cash book balance.

Since you attended the lesson, write a
summary of what you would explain to
her.

Starting with the bank statement
balance

When starting with the bank statement
balance, add with uncredited cheque
and less with unpresented cheque. This
time around, the uncredited cheques are
added. This is because the uncredited
cheques causes the bank statement to
report a lower balance than the cash book.

On the other hand, unpresented cheques
will be deducted from the bank statement
balance to arrive at the adjusted cash
book balance, because the unpresented
cheques cause the bank statement to have
a higher balance than the cash book.
