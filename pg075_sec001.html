Student’s Book Form Two

Correction of Book-keeping errors and the Trial balance

Required:

From the given transactions, make journal entries to correct the errors and prepare
the suspense account.

Correcting a combination of errors with different effects on the trial balance agreement

In real-life situation, there would be a combination of different types of errors
discovered from the books of accounts. Some of the errors may be affecting the trial
balance agreement, while some would not.

Example 2.13

A book-keeper extracted a trial balance on 31st December 2024. This failed to agree by TZS 399,000 shortage on the credit column of the trial balance. A suspense account was opened for the difference. In January 2025 the following errors made in 2024 were found.

(i)

Sales day book was undercast by TZS 320,000.

(ii)

A receipt of TZS 380,000 from Masesa, a debtor was recorded only in the cash

account, with no corresponding entry to the trade receivables account.

(iii) Wages account was undercast by TZS 171,000.

(iv) Sales of TZS 1,159,000 to Emma was debited in error to Enea’s account.

(v)

Discounts allowed account was overcast by TZS 190,000.

(vi) The amount of interest received was erroneously recorded in the commission
received account TZS 437,000.

Required:

(a)

Show the journal entries necessary to correct the errors.

(b)

Prepare the suspense account and post relevant entries.
