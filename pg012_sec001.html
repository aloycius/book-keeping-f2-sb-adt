From Monica’s cash account, you must have noticed that the folio column has not been used, except for showing whether the balance indicated is a balance carried down (c/d) or a balance brought down (b/d).

At this stage, it is important one could properly employ different references that are used in the folio column of the two- column and the three-column cash books. The following folio references will be used for the purpose of this book, where relevant:

C – Contra entry

SL – Sales ledger

GL – General ledger

PL – Purchases ledger
