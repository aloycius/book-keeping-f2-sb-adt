(a) The two items highlighted in green

appear on the debit column of the bank

statement but are missing on the credit

side of the cash book. These represent

direct debits made by the bank, and
form part of adjusting items.

(b) The item highlighted in gray appears

on the credit column of the bank

statement but is missing on the cash

book (debit side). This is a direct

credit, which also form part of the

adjusting items.

(c) The items highlighted in red represent

errors, as they have different figures

appearing in the cash book from the one

appearing on the bank statement. These

on the cash book or notifying the bank

to correct its records, depending on

which record is erroneous.

an uncredited a cheque.

(e) The items highlighted in blue represent

a dishonoured cheque.
