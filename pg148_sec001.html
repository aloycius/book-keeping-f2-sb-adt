Chapter Summary

1. A financial budget is a plan that outlines how money will be generated and spent over a specific period. 2. A financial budget helps individuals and businesses manage their finances wisely to avoid shortages. 3. Budgeting is the process of estimating future revenues and expenditures to ensure financial stability. 4. Budgeting control involves monitoring and adjusting the budget to prevent overspending or deficits. 5. A well-prepared budget avoids financial shortfalls. It should be realistic based on actual revenues and expenses. It should be flexible,
