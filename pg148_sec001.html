Chapter Summary

1.
A financial budget is a plan that

outlines how money will be

generated and spent over a specific
period.

2.
A financial budget helps individuals

and businesses manage their finances

wisely to avoid shortages.

3.
Budgeting is the process of estimating

future revenues and expenditures to

ensure financial stability.

4.
Budgeting control involves

monitoring and adjusting the budget

to prevent overspending or deficits.

5. A well-prepared budget avoids

financial shortfalls. It should be

realistic based on actual revenues

and expenses. It should be flexible,

allowing adjustments when

unexpected financial changes occur.

Additionally, it must be clear, goal-
oriented, and time-bound to ensure
effective financial planning.

6.
Budgeting allows businesses to

plan for the future by organising

their revenues and expenses. It also
helps in decision-making, ensuring

that money is used efficiently and

minimises financial risks.

7.
When preparing a cash budget, a
business must estimate all its cash

receipts and disbursements over a

set period to ensure it can meet its

financial obligations and avoid cash

shortages.
