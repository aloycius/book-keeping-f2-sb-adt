Use of unadjusted cash book balance for reconciling overdraft situation

It is important to remember that a bank reconciliation statement can be prepared without adjusting the cash book. The same procedure is followed when dealing with an overdraft, either in a cash book or a bank statement. As learnt before, a bank reconciliation statement can be prepared by either not reversing the reconciling items or reversing them.

Reconciliation without reversing the reconciling items This reconciliation can be performed by expressing the overdrafts in terms of negative numbers. The reconciling items will retain their character in terms of their effect on relationship between the cash book and the bank statement balance. The reconciliation can thus be made in the usual way.
