Preparation of the petty cash book For a proper record of different expenses in the petty cash book, it is important to organise the book logically. The best way to organise it is to make a column for each major category of repetitive transactions of the petty cash. There may therefore be a column for transport, stationeries, refreshments. A typical page in a petty cash book can be prepared as follows: Format of petty cash book for the month

Note: PCV – Petty cash voucher The petty cash book has separate column assigned to each specific category of expenses. These columns are called analytical expense columns. It also has a column for other expenses, which is used to record expenses that do not fall within any of the analytical columns. When entering a transaction in an analytical expense column, the petty cashier also records the same in the total column. The identity of the source document, the petty cash voucher (PCV) number, is recorded in the specific column.
