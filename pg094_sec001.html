Student’s Book Form Two

12. Y our book-keeper extracted a trial balance on 31 st December 2023, which failed to agree by TZS 159,600 a shortage on the credit column of the trial balance. A suspense account was opened for the difference. During January 2024 the following errors were found.

(i) Sales account was undercast by TZS 152,000.

(ii) Salaries account was undercast by TZS 68,400.

(iii) Sales of goods for TZS 463,600 to Regina was incorrectly debited to Renada’s account.

(iv) Discounts allowed account was overcast by TZS 76,000.

(v) TZS 174,800 amount of interest received was incorrectly recorded in the rent income account.

Required:

(a) Show the journal entries necessary to correct the errors.

(b) Draw the suspense account and post the relevant entries.

13. Jumbe extracted a trial balance from her book-keeping records on 30 th June 2023. Unfortunately, the trial balance failed to balance and the difference was TZS 370,500, a debit balance placed in the suspense account. The following errors were later found:

(i) A cheque payment of TZS 1,657,500 for general expenses had been entered in the cash book but no corresponding entry had been made in the general expenses account.

(ii) A payment for wages of TZS 1,696,500 by cheque had been correctly entered in the cash book but was shown as TZS 1,521,000 in the wages account.

(iii) The returns inwards account had been overcast by TZS 1,950,000.

(iv) Commission received of TZS 487,500 had been entered twice in the commission income account.

(v) The account of Jamal was incorrectly credited in respect of goods purchased on credit from Jamila for TZS 6,240,000.

(vi) Payment for telephone expenses of TZS 936,000 had been debited to both the telephone expenses account and the bank account.

(vii) TZS 1,072,500 paid for stationeries had not been entered in the stationeries expenses account.

Required: Show the entries in general journal and postings to suspense account to correct the given errors.
