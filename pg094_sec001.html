13. Jumbe extracted a trial balance from her book-keeping records on 30th June 2023.

Unfortunately, the trial balance failed to balance and the difference was TZS

370,500, a debit balance placed in the suspense account. The following errors

were later found:

(i) A cheque payment of TZS 1,657,500 for general expenses had been entered

in the cash book but no corresponding entry had been made in the general
expenses account.

(ii)
A payment for wages of TZS 1,696,500 by cheque had been correctly entered

in the cash book but was shown as TZS 1,521,000 in the wages account.

(iii) The returns inwards account had been overcast by TZS 1,950,000.

(iv) Commission received of TZS 487,500 had been entered twice in the commission

income account.

(v)
The account of Jamal was incorrectly credited in respect of goods purchased

on credit from Jamila for TZS 6,240,000.

(vi)
Payment for telephone expenses of TZS 936,000 had been debited to both the

telephone expenses account and the bank account.

(vii) TZS 1,072,500 paid for stationeries had not been entered in the stationeries

expenses account.

Required:

Show the entries in general journal and postings to suspense account to correct
the given errors.
