8. Mangungu enterprises had a normal cash book balance of TZS 786,000 in the bank account on 30th June 2024, and the bank statement showed a credit balance of TZS 1,378,000 after comparing the cash book with the bank statement. The following differences had been observed:

(a) Uncredited cheque of TZS 152,000.

(b) Unpresented cheque of TZS 568,000.

(c) Bank charges TZS 10,000 appeared on the bank statement but not in the cash
book.

(d) A standing order of TZS 10,000 payable on 28th June had been paid by the bank but no entry had been made in the cash book.

(e) Interest of TZS 196,000 had been credited by the bank to Mangungu’s current account but no entry had been made in the cash book.

Required:

Prepare a bank reconciliation statement as at 30th June 2024 starting with balance as per cash book.

9. On 31st December 2024, the cash book balance of Mapesa was TZS 3,044,400

(Dr) whereas the bank statement showed a balance of TZS 3,080,400 (Cr). After
comparing these two balances, the following were discovered:

(a) A cheque not yet presented for payment TZS 1,480,800.

(b) A cheque deposited into the bank but not yet credited by the bank TZS

1,459,200.

(c) Items shown in the bank statement but not yet entered in the cash book were

as follows: Bank charges TZS 28,800. Standing order TZS 55,200. Dividend

collected by the bank TZS 98,400.

Required:

(i)

Prepare an adjusted cash book to show the correct cash book balance.

(ii)

Prepare a bank reconciliation statement starting with the adjusted cash book

balance.
