Correcting an error of complete reversal of entries

Correcting this error needs to make entries that will: (i) offset the records made on the wrong sides; and (ii) record the transaction on the appropriate sides.

This will require debiting the account that was credited with an amount equal to two times the amount credited. It will also require crediting the account that was debited with an amount equal to two times the amount debited. Example 2.6 illustrates the correction of this type of error.

Example 2.6

Purchase of goods on credit for TZS 600,000 was debited to trade payables account and credited to purchases account.

What has been done to record the transaction is exactly the opposite of what should have been done. So, if TZS 600,000 is only debited to purchases account and credited to trade payables account, what has been recorded will be cancelled. This will leave the transaction unrecorded. Therefore, there is a need to make another TZS 600,000 debit to purchases account and TZS 600,000 credit to trade payables account.
