Discount allowed and discount received

The discussion on discounts indicates that discounts can be given by the business or given to the business. Therefore, there is a need to distinguish between discount allowed and discount received.

Discount allowed is the reduction of the invoice price allowed to business credit customers who pay within the discount period specified on the invoices. On the other hand, discount received is the reduction given to the business by its suppliers when the business pays amounts due within the discount period specified on the suppliers’ invoices, for example, paying for purchases.
