In this case, the suspense account balance is not eliminated. When this situation exists during the time of financial statements preparation, the balance is reported in the statement of financial position. If the suspense account has a credit balance, like this case of Mwanampiga, the balance is presented as a current liability in the statement of financial position. In case of a debit balance, the statement of financial position will show it as a current asset.

Correcting errors that eliminate the suspense account

Errors can be detected and corrected in a way that they eliminate the suspense account. A suspense account is eliminated when it has a zero balance. These errors are corrected following the same rules for correcting different types of errors. The elimination of the suspense account does not mean that all errors have been discovered and corrected. Example 2.12 elaborates on elimination of suspense account.

Example 2.12

The trial balance of Evansi at 30 th June 2023 showed the total of debit column was TZS 2,973,000 higher than the credit column total. The difference was placed in the suspense account and the following errors were found:

(i) The sales account was overcast by TZS 225,000. (ii) Cash received from Chakubanga, a debtor, in full settlement of his account of TZS 450,000 was correctly posted in cash account but omitted in the trade receivables account. (iii) Purchase of goods on credit from Masika amounting to TZS 375,000 has been credited in the sales account. This was correctly recorded in the trade payables account.
