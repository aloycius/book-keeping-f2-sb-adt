Student’s Book Form Two

Starting with the bank statement balance

When starting with bank statement balance, direct debits such as standing orders and bank charges are added, because they cause the bank statement to have a lower balance than the unadjusted cash book. On the other hand, direct credits such as interest received and credit transfer are deducted, because they make the bank statement balance higher than the unadjusted cash book balance. Similarly, the unpresented cheques are added and uncredited cheques are subtracted.
