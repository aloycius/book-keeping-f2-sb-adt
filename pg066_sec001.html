Correcting compensating errors

Since these are two or more errors, each error is corrected separately by debiting the
account which has an excessive credit balance as a result of the error, and crediting the
account that has an excessive debit balance as a result of the error. As long as they both
involve the same amount and the errors are made on the opposite sides of accounts, the
correct entry will obviously be a double entry. The following examples demonstrate
how you can correct compensating errors.

Example 2.7

The balance of sales account was overstated by TZS 120,000. The wages account
was overcast by TZS 120,000. It is a coincidence that the same amount is involved in
the two errors, both overstating the accounts, where one has a normal credit balance,
and the other has a normal debit balance. To correct this error, sales account is debited
by TZS 120,000 and wages account is credited by TZS 120,000.

Being compensating error now corrected

Example 2.8

The purchases day book has been undercasted by TZS 210,000. In this error, both
the purchases account and the trade payables account have been understated by TZS

210,000. To correct this, there is a need to make debit entry of TZS 210,000 in the
purchases account and a credit entry of TZS 210,000 in the trade payables account.
