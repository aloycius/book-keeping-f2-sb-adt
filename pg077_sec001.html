Correction of Book-keeping errors and the Trial balance

Exercise 2.5

The following errors were discovered in the books of Sijaona retailer after the trial balance extracted on 31st December 2024 had failed to agree, with the debit column exceeding the credit column by TZS 2,500,000:

(i)

Additional capital of TZS 1,000,000 paid into the bank had been credited to

sales account.

(ii)

Goods taken by Sijaona for own use of TZS 500,000 was debited to general

expenses.

(iii)

A purchase of goods from Katete of TZS 8,570,000 was entered in the books

as TZS 5,870,000.

(iv)

Sales account was overcast by TZS 7,000,000.

(v)

Rent expense account was undercast by TZS 4,000,000.

(vi)

Cash received from a debtor entered in the cash book only, TZS 5,000,000.

(vii)

Cash purchases of goods TZS 590,000 entered in the books, both debit and

credit entries as TZS 950,000.

(viii)

Purchases account was undercast by TZS 8,500,000.

Required:

(a) Show the necessary journal entries to correct the given errors.

(b) Prepare the suspense account.

Adjusting the trial balance

An adjusted trial balance is a trial balance that contains updated balances after correcting
all the errors. This is prepared after all the entries made to correct errors have been posted
to the ledgers and new balances obtained. In case there were some errors affecting the
trial balance agreement, the suspense account would be opened and entries posted to it.

In real life, there would be several revisions of the trial balance after the original trial
balance has been prepared.
