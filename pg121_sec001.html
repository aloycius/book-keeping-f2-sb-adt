Reconciliation in overdraft situation

There are situations where at the end
of the month the business will have an
overdrawn balance with the bank. It may
happen that both the cash book and the
bank statement show an overdraft at the
particular date. It may also happen that
either the cash book or the bank statement
shows an overdraft, while the other shows a
favourable balance. Whatever the situation,
the bank reconciliation statement can be
prepared to reconcile the two balances
either by adjusting the cash book or without
adjusting the cash book. The approach is
the same as learnt for favourable balances,
only that it is important to be sure the
balances should be treated appropriately
as overdrawn balances. This is because the
reconciling and adjusting items will have a
different effect on overdrafts (negative or
unfavourable balance) as compared with
the effect they have on favourable balances.
