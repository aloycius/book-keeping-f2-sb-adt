Activity 3.1

Visit commercial bank in your locality, ask how the bank officers prepare bank statements, and the reasons for the preparation of the bank statements.

Comparing the cash book and the bank statement A comparison can be made between the cash book (bank column) and the bank statements. The approach starts with the ideal situation where there are no timing differences for items recorded in either the cash book (bank column) or the bank statement at the time of comparison. In this situation, each item on the credit side of the cash book is reflected on the debit column of the bank statement. Similarly, each item on the debit side of the cash book appears on the credit column of the bank statement. Therefore, comparison must be made between:

(a) The credit side of the cash book against the debit column of the bank statement.

(b) The debit side of the cash book against the credit column of the bank statement.
