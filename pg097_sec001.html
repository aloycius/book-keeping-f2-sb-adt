Chapter Three

Reconciling bank account with cash book balances

Introduction

Businesses that conduct transactions through the banking systems need to have a system to constantly check the records of their bank transactions. This is facilitated by periodically comparing the bank column of their cash book against the statement received from the bank with details of transactions on their bank account. For any differences in entries and balances, an appropriate explanation will have to be sought and clarified. In this chapter, you will learn the bank reconciliation statement and adjusted cash book. The competencies developed will enable you to describe bank reconciliation statement, compare bank statement and cash book, make appropriate adjustments in the cash book, and prepare a bank reconciliation statement.

Bank reconciliation statement

A bank reconciliation statement is a statement prepared for the purpose of reconciling the bank balance as per cash book and the bank balance as per bank statement. A typical bank reconciliation statement begins with the cash book balance, adds and subtracts the relevant

reconciling items and ends with the bank statement balance. The statement can also start with the bank statement balance and finish with the cash book balance. The actual structure of a bank reconciliation statement is illustrated in the next sections.
