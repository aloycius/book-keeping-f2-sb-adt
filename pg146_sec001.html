Student’s Book Form Two

Example 4.1

A business owner expected to start the month with cash balance of TZS 5,000,000. The expected monthly revenue sources that will involve cash collections includes a salary of TZS 25,000,000 and a business income of TZS 3,000,000. The estimated monthly expenses are rent TZS 8,000,000, food TZS 5,000,000, transport TZS 2,000,000, and entertainment TZS 3,000,000. In addition, the business owner plans to spend TZS 6,000,000 to acquire office furniture. Prepare a cash budget and determine the closing balance for the month.

Solution

A cash budget for the month

Item Amount (TZS)
Opening cash balance 5,000,000
Cash receipts:
Salary 25,000,000
Business income 3,000,000
Total cash receipts 28,000,000
Less: Disbursements
Rent 8,000,000
Food 5,000,000
Transport 2,000,000
Entertainment 3,000,000
Furniture 6,000,000
Total disbursements 24,000,000
Budgeted net cash flow 4,000,000
Closing cash balance 9,000,000

Prepare a cash budget and determine the closing balance for the period.
