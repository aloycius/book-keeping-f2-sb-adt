Exercise 1.5

Triple D is a sole trader who maintains her petty cash book on the imprest system. Petty cash float for the business is set at TZS 360,000. On 1 st July 2023, the petty cash balance was TZS 216,000. On that date, she withdrew money from the bank to restore balance to the imprest amount. During July 2023, she made the following transactions.

15/07/2023 Paid for cleaning materials PCV No. 004 18,000

20/07/2023 Paid for petrol PCV No. 006 25,740

23/07/2023 Paid postage costs PCV No. 007 12,060

28/07/2023 Paid for purchase of postage stamps PCV No. 010 13,860

29/07/2023 Paid for diesel PCV No. 011 26,640

Triple D used the following analysis columns: Postage, Travelling expenses, Motor expenses, Cleaning expenses and Ledger accounts.
