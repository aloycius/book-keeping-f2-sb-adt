Three-column cash book

A three-column cash book is a cash book that is prepared with three amount columns on
both sides (debit and the credit). It has one additional column from the two-column cash
book. The third column is designed to record cash discount. The three-column cash
book is, therefore, a cash book with three amount columns, namely: the cash column,
bank column and discount column. When a business has more than one bank account,
each account will have a separate column, but the cash book will still be known as the
three-column cash book.
