Student’s Book Form Two

Reconciling bank account with cash book balances

Starting with unadjusted cash book balance When starting with the unadjusted cash book balance, unpresented cheques and direct credits are added. This is because direct credits have been credited in the bank statement, but not debited in the cash book. These items, thus, cause the cash book balance to be lower than in the bank statement.

The uncredited cheques, the bank charges, standing orders and other direct debits need to be deducted. This is because such items have been debited in the bank statement but not yet credited in the cash book. At any point in time the cash book balance will be higher than the bank statement balance if there are any direct debits in the bank account.
