Example 1.9

Mkombozi is a businessman who maintains his petty cash book with a petty cash float of TZS 600,000. The following is a list of his petty cash transactions for the month of January 2024.

TZS 1/01/2024 Received a petty cash float from chief cashier

2/01/2024 Bought sugar for office, PCV No. 501 36,000

4/01/2024 Paid travelling expenses, PCV No. 502 24,000

5/01/2024 Paid office cleaner, PCV No. 503 30,000

8/01/2024 Bought petrol for office van PCV No. 504 72,000

9/01/2024 Paid for travelling expenses, PCV No. 505 50,000

10/01/2024 Bought note books for staff, PCV No. 506 34,000

12/01/2024 Paid window cleaner, PCV No. 507 36,000

15/01/2024 Bought milk for staff, PCV No. 508 10,000

18/01/2024 Paid bus fare for staff, PCV No. 509 16,000

19/01/2024 Paid stationeries expenses, PCV No. 510 18,000

20/01/2024 Paid for cleaning materials, PCV No. 511 88,000

25/01/2024 Bought sugar and tea leaves, PCV No. 512 36,000

26/01/2024 Paid diesel for delivery van, PCV No. 513 122,000

1/02/2024 Received re-imbursement from the chief cashier for the amount spent in January

You are required to record a petty cash book analysed into columns for expenditure on cleaning, motor expenses, stationeries, travelling and other expenses. Enter the month’s transactions, balance and carry down the balance and record the reimbursement done on 1 st February 2024.
