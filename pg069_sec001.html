Correction of book-keeping errors that

affect trial balance agreement

These errors are unilateral; meaning that
the error is made in one of the accounts
involved in recording the transaction.

In case, it is a casting error in balancing
an account, the entry to correct will be
crediting (provided that the casting error
increased debit balance or reduced a credit
balance). The vice versa is also true.

The entry required to correct an error
which affects trial balance does not
necessarily require double entry as was
the case with errors that do not affect
trial balance agreement. Instead, the
corresponding entries for these errors are
made in a temporary account opened for
special purpose. This account is called a
suspense account.
