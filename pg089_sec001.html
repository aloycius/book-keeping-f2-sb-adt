In addition to the mistakes evident in the trial balance given, the following
errors were also discovered:

(i)

A payment of TZS 409,500 made to a creditor had been posted to the cash

book only.

(ii)

A cheque for TZS 244,400 received from a customer had been correctly

posted to the cash book but posted to the trade receivables as TZS 234,000.

(iii)

A purchase of shelves for the shop of TZS 529,100 had been included in

the purchases account.

(iv)

A TZS 54,600 total of the discounts allowed column in the cash book had

not been posted to the general ledger.

(v)

A page of the sales day book was correctly totalled as TZS 994,500 but

carried forward as TZS 877,500.

Required:

Show the trial balance as it would appear after correcting the discovered errors.
