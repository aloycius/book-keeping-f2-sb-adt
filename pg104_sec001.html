Reasons for inconsistencies of cash book and bank statement

Cash book (bank column) and bank statement are supposed to give identical

balances at a given date because they both record the same transactions. However, in reality the two are unlikely to have same balance due to some reasons. The following are the reasons for the inconsistencies between cash book and bank statement balance.
