Reasons for inconsistencies of cash
book and bank statement

Cash book (bank column) and bank
statement are supposed to give identical

balances at a given date because they both
record the same transactions. However,
in reality the two are unlikely to have
same balance due to some reasons.

The following are the reasons for the
inconsistencies between cash book and
bank statement balance.

Unpresented or outstanding cheques

An unpresented cheque is a cheque which
the payee has not yet presented to the
bank for payment. Cheques issued by the
business during the month and not yet
presented for payment within the month
