In addition to the evident mistakes, the following errors were also discovered:

(i)

A payment of TZS 1,023,750 made to a creditor was posted to cash book, with

no corresponding entry to the trade payables account.

(ii) A cheque of TZS 611,000 received from a customer was correctly entered in
the cash book but posted to the trade receivables account as TZS 585,000.

(iii) Furniture bought for office use of TZS 1,322,750 was included in the purchases account.

(iv) The total in discounts allowed column of the cash book of TZS 136,500 was
not posted to the general ledger.

(v)
A page of the sales day book was correctly totalled as TZS 2,193,750 but
posted to the trade receivables account as TZS 2,486,250.

Required:

Show how the trial balance would appear after correcting discovered errors.
