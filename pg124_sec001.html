Reversing the reconciling items

Starting with the adjusted cash book balance

Starting with adjusted balance (overdraft) as per cash book, the reconciling items can be reversed, and a bank reconciliation statement can be prepared. This is done when one wants to avoid using negative balances to represent overdraft in the bank reconciliation statement. To be able to do this, the relationship between the reconciling items and the balances is re-examined. The unpresented cheques make the cash book balance lower than the bank statement balance, and their effect on the overdraft will be the opposite. This is because unpresented cheques will cause a higher amount of overdraft in the cash book than the one on the bank statement. On the other hand, the uncredited cheques will cause the cash book to have a lower figure of overdraft than the one on the bank statement.
