Example 2.9

One page of sales returns journal has been carried forward as TZS 310,000 instead of TZS 130,000. This error results in an overcast in the sales return journal. This means that the debit entry posted to the sales returns account and the credit entry made to trade receivables account is TZS 180,000 (i.e. TZS 310,000 – TZS 130,000) higher than what it should have been. Its effect is that the sales returns account is overcast by TZS 180,000, and the trade receivables account is undercast by TZS 180,000. This needs a debit entry of TZS 180,000 in the trade receivables account and a credit entry of TZS 180,000 to the sales returns account.
