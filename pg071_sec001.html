Student’s Book Form Two

Correction of Book-keeping errors and the Trial balance

Example 2.11

Mwanampiga extracted a trial balance from his book-keeping records on 30 th June 2023. The trial balance failed to agree and the difference was TZS 247,000, a shortage on the debit column. This was placed to a suspense account. The following errors were later found:

(i) A TZS 1,105,000 cheque payment for general expenses had been entered in the cash book but no entry had been made in the general expenses account. (ii) A cheque payment for wages TZS 1,131,000 had been correctly entered in the cash book but was entered as TZS 1,031,000 in the wages account. (iii) Commission received of TZS 325,000 had been entered twice in the commission income account. (iv) A cheque payment for telephone expenses TZS 624,000 had been debited to both the telephone expenses account and the bank account. (v) Stationeries expenses TZS 715,000 had not been entered in the stationeries expenses account.

Enter the given transactions in the general journal (ignore narrations) and post to the suspense account to correct the errors.
