When the trial balance totals fail to agree,
a balance is inserted temporarily in the
side with a lower amount of total. Doing
so will make the total debit balance and
total credit balance to be the same. For
example, when the total credit balances
is TZS 10,200,000 and the total debit
balances is TZS 9,750,000, a debit balance
of TZS 450,000 is inserted to make both
totals TZS 10,200,000. The account name
given to this balance is suspense account.

When errors that affect the trial balance
are corrected, double entry is completed
in the suspense account. When all errors
have been corrected, the balance in the

suspense account will be zero. For this
reason, a suspense account balance will
no longer appear in the trial balance.

It may also happen that not all errors

that affect the trial balance agreement
have been discovered and corrected at
the time when financial statements are
being prepared. In this case, the suspense
account would still have a balance. When
the suspense account exists up to the date

of preparing the financial statements,
the balance would be reflected in the
statement of financial position. The
balance will be shown as a current asset
or current liability, depending on whether
it is a debit balance or a credit balance.

Visit the school library or use online
sources to search for information about
the suspense account and identify
errors that may lead to its preparation.

Correction of errors using the suspense
account

Journal entries can be used to correct
book-keeping errors. These entries are
subsequently posted to respective ledger
accounts, and in case of errors affecting
the trial balance agreement, the ledger
accounts in question include the suspense
account. When doing this, there are
two possible results. One is a situation
where the suspense account balance is
not eliminated by the correction of book-
keeping errors. Another is a situation

where the suspense account will be
eliminated after posting the correcting
entries.

Suspense account not eliminated

This is a situation where the correction
of errors, including those affecting the
trial balance agreement, has not been
completed. The situation therefore calls
for continued errors identification and

correction. Example 2.11 illustrates the
suspense account is not eliminated.
