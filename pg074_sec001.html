Exercise 2.4

Amina Mtindo extracted a trial balance from her book-keeping records on 31 st January 2025. The trial balance failed to balance and the difference of TZS 2,036,000 is placed as credit balance in the suspense account. The following errors were discovered:

(i) Purchases account in the ledger was undercast by TZS 1,000,000.

(ii) A total of TZS 1,500,000 in the sales journal is correctly posted to the sales account, but posted to the trade receivables account as TZS 2,500,000.

(iii) Purchases journal totalling TZS 12,000,000 had been correctly posted to the purchases account, but posted to trade payables account as TZS 10,000,000.
