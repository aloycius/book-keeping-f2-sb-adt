Items in (a) and (b) with the exception
of dishonoured cheques are referred to
as reconciling items. Items in (c) and

(d), as well as dishonoured cheques are
referred to as adjusting items. Items under

(e) represent items that would require
correction in either the cash book or the
bank statement, depending on which of the
figures is correct. The correct figure would
be discovered by tracking the transaction to
the source documents. Example 3.1 shows
how the cash book and bank statement
record bank transactions of the business.
