Student’s Book Form Two

Reconciling bank account with cash book balances

The template for bank reconciliation statement, will be prepared as follows:

Bank reconciliation statement as at…………..

Amount
Adjusted balance (overdraft) as per cash book xxx
Add: Uncredited cheques / deposits in transit/ uncleared deposits xxx
xxx
Less: Unpresented cheques/ outstanding cheques xxx
Balance (overdraft) as per bank statement xxx

Using data from example 3.4, the bank reconciliation statement for Zena Kisogo can be prepared as follows:

Bank reconciliation statement as at 31 st March 2024

TZS
Overdraft as per adjusted cash book 17,535,500
Add: Uncredited cheques 5,720,000
23,255,500
Less: Unpresented cheques 4,350,000
Overdraft as per bank statement 18,905,500

Starting with overdraft as per bank statement

When starting with the overdraft as per bank statement, it requires to add the unpresented cheques as they cause the bank statement to report a lower amount of overdraft than the cash book. On the other hand, the uncredited cheques will have to be subtracted as they cause the bank statement to have a higher amount of overdraft.
