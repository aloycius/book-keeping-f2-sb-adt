Note: The bank balance shown in the financial statements is the adjusted cash book

balance. This is because the reconciling items will eventually be settled. The cheques
already issued are out of the control of the business and it is a matter of time before they
are presented for payment, unless for some valid reasons the business issues a ‘stop
payment order’. As for uncredited cheques, the bank will clear such cheques with the
respective drawers.

Visit any nearby company, consult the accountant and request to collect various
bank reconciliation statements they have prepared. Observe and analyse those
statements to see how they have been prepared, and then write a summary of what
you have observed.
