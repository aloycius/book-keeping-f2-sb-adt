Student’s Book Form Two

Reconciling bank account with cash book balances

Exercise 3.7

On 30 th November 2023, Katani Secondary School’s cash book (bank column) showed a credit balance of TZS 4,680,000. From the following information adjust the cash book and calculate the bank statement balance.

(a) Items shown in the bank statement but not yet entered in the cash book TZS

Standing order: Insurance 560,000

Bank charges 28,500

Dishonoured cheques:

Issued to Edward 234,000

Received from Mtalima 849,500

(b) Cheques drawn but not yet presented to the bank:

Mchanga 85,500

Nyakaro 147,000

(c) Cheque payment of TZS 162,000 had been entered in the cash book (bank column) as TZS 135,000

(d) Cheques deposited into the bank but not yet created by the bank

Kasubi 300,000

Asia 820,500

Note: The bank balance shown in the financial statements is the adjusted cash book balance. This is because the reconciling items will eventually be settled. The cheques already issued are out of the control of the business and it is a matter of time before they are presented for payment, unless for some valid reasons the business issues a ‘stop payment order’. As for uncredited cheques, the bank will clear such cheques with the respective drawers.
