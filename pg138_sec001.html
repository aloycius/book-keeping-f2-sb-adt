Concept of budgeting and budgetary control

Budgeting and budgetary control are essential tools for managing finances in businesses. A budget is a financial plan that outlines expected revenue and costs. Budgetary control ensures that the actual spending of a business aligns with the budget. This helps businesses to allocate their financial resources, avoid overspending, and achieve financial stability. A good budget should be realistic, clear, flexible and goal-oriented to support effective decision-making. Different budgeting approaches, such as incremental and zero-based, allow

businesses to plan based on their financial goals. Understanding the features of a good budget and budgeting approaches enables enterprises to manage cash flow efficiently, reduce financial risks, and grow sustainably.
