Concept of budgeting and budgetary

control

Budgeting and budgetary control are
essential tools for managing finances
in businesses. A budget is a financial
plan that outlines expected revenue and
costs. Budgetary control ensures that
the actual spending of a business aligns
with the budget. This helps businesses to
allocate their financial resources, avoid
overspending, and achieve financial

stability. A good budget should be

realistic, clear, flexible and goal-oriented

to support effective decision-making.

Different budgeting approaches, such
as incremental and zero-based, allow

businesses to plan based on their financial
goals. Understanding the features of a
good budget and budgeting approaches
enables enterprises to manage cash flow
efficiently, reduce financial risks, and
grow sustainably.

Introduction

Managing resources effectively is important for small businesses to keep the
business running smoothly. A cash budget helps business owners plan for the money
flowing in and out, ensuring they always have enough cash to pay for expenses,
such as rent, salaries and supplies. The budget helps business owners to make
better financial decisions, avoid cash shortages, and stabilise their businesses.

In this chapter, you will learn budgeting, budgetary control, and preparation of
a simple cash budget. The competencies developed will enable you to prepare a
simple cash budget.
