Preparing a bank reconciliation statement in an overdraft situation

As was the case with favourable balances, reconciliation can be done starting by either
the cash book balance or the bank statement balance. It is important to note that either
the adjusted cash book balance (overdraft) or the unadjusted one can be used. One
peculiar issue is that it is an overdraft, or an unfavourable balance. Therefore, the
effect of the reconciling items on the relationship between the overdraft as per cash
book and the overdraft as per bank statement will not be the same as it would be in
case of favourable balances. The effects can be summarised as follows:

(i)

Items that normally cause the cash book balance to be lower than the bank statement

balance will cause the cash book to have a higher amount of overdraft than the
bank statement.

(ii) Items that cause the cash book balance to be higher than the bank statement

balance will cause the cash book to have a lower amount of overdraft than the
bank statement.

This involves a different treatment of the reconciling items when preparing the bank
reconciliation statement. It can be done either without reversing the reconciling items
or by reversing the reconciling items.
