5. Errors related to misplacing ledger balances in the trial balance This error may happen when extracting the trial balance. A debit balance extracted from the ledger is taken to the credit column of the trial balance, or the vice versa. An example is where a correct balance of sales account is placed in the debit column of the trial balance. The trial balance will not agree unless there is a coincidence that another balance of the same amount is also erroneously placed on the credit column.

General approach to undertaking correction of book-keeping errors

Deliberate efforts should be made to identify book-keeping errors in the

accounting records. Once identified, the errors should be corrected. The general approach to correcting errors requires the following to be done in respect of each error:

(i) Identify the correct entries that should have been made for the transaction;

(ii) Find out the entries that were made;

(iii) Make journal entries to make necessary correction(s).

The correct entry to be made will involve a double entry whose effect is to both offset the erroneous entry made and make the correct entry. After the correction, the books should show a position as if the correct entry had been made in the first place. For the purpose of this book, the focus will be on correcting general ledger balances, except for situations where an error affects only the subsidiary ledger, for example, when correcting an error of commission.
