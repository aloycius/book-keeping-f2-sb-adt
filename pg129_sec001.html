Student’s Book Form Two

Reconciling bank account with cash book balances

Reversing the reconciling items Starting with the overdraft as per cash book

The reconciling items are reversed for an obvious reason. Items that would have caused cash book balance to be higher than bank statement balance would make the overdraft as per cash book lower than the overdraft as per bank statement. The effects of adjusting items on the overdraft are the opposite of the effects that such items would have on a favourable balance. The bank reconciliation statement is prepared as follows:
