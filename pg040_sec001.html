Student’s Book Form Two

The receipt column records the receipt of
cash, normally petty cash reimbursement.

This is done either at the end of each petty
cash period, or after spending the whole
amount of the petty cash imprest.

Recording transactions and balancing
the petty cash book

This involves recording the following:

(a) Receipt of the petty cash float from the main cashier

This is recorded on the receipts

column of the petty cash book, and

its corresponding entry is made on the
payments side of the main cash book.

(b) Making different payments from the
petty cash

Each payment is recorded on the

appropriate analytical expense column.

A record is also made on the total

column on the payments side of the

petty cash book.

(c) Receipt of reimbursement from the main cashier

This is recorded on the receipt side of the

petty cash book, and its corresponding

entry is made on the credit side of the

main cash book. Reimbursement can be

made at the end of the imprest period
or at the beginning of the next imprest
period.

(d) Balancing of the petty cash book

The petty cash book is balanced in the
same manner as any other cash book.

An important thing to note is that the

petty cashier should balance the receipts

column against the total column of the

payment side. If the reimbursement is
done at the end of the imprest period,
the balance of the petty cash book will

be equal to the imprest amount. This is

because recording the reimbursement

will restore the balance to the imprest
amount. On the other hand, when the

reimbursement is done at the beginning

of the following imprest period, the

balance of the petty cash book at the
end of the period will be the amount
of imprest not spent during the period.

This will be restored at the beginning

of the next period before petty cash

payments are made for that period.

the imprest at the end of the period.
