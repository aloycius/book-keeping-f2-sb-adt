Student’s Book Form Two

The receipt column records the receipt of cash, normally petty cash reimbursement. This is done either at the end of each petty cash period, or after spending the whole amount of the petty cash imprest.

Recording transactions and balancing the petty cash book This involves recording the following: (a) Receipt of the petty cash float from the main cashier This is recorded on the receipts column of the petty cash book, and its corresponding entry is made on the payments side of the main cash book. (b) Making different payments from the petty cash Each payment is recorded on the appropriate analytical expense column. A record is also made on the total column on the payments side of the petty cash book. (c) Receipt of reimbursement from the main cashier This is recorded on the receipt side of the petty cash book, and its corresponding entry is made on the credit side of the main cash book. Reimbursement can be made at the end of the imprest period or at the beginning of the next imprest period. (d) Balancing of the petty cash book The petty cash book is balanced in the same manner as any other cash book. An important thing to note is that the petty cashier should balance the receipts column against the total column of the payment side. If the reimbursement is done at the end of the imprest period, the balance of the petty cash book will be equal to the imprest amount. This is because recording the reimbursement

will restore the balance to the imprest amount. On the other hand, when the reimbursement is done at the beginning of the following imprest period, the balance of the petty cash book at the end of the period will be the amount of imprest not spent during the period. This will be restored at the beginning of the next period before petty cash payments are made for that period. Example 1.8 shows reimbursement of the imprest at the end of the period.

Example 1.8

Pongezi traders’ business maintains its petty cash book on the imprest system, the imprest being TZS 750,000 per week. The following are petty cash transactions for the first week of May 2023:

TZS 01/05/2023 Petty cash balance 750,000 02/05/2023 Bought stationeries 270,000 03/05/2023 Paid Jitihada, a creditor 90,000

04/05/2023 Paid transport charges 150,000 05/05/2023 Bought pens and rulers for office use 130,000

06/05/2023 Paid meal allowance for staff 100,000

07/05/2023 Received from the chief cashier a reimbursement for the amount used in the period

Record the given transactions in the petty cash book and balance as at 7 th May 2023 then bring down the balance. The analysis columns used by Pongezi traders are as follows: stationeries, transport, meal allowances and ledger accounts.
