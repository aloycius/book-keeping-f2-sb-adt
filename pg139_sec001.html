Budgetary control refers to setting financial targets, comparing actual performance with the budget, and taking corrective actions to ensure business goals are met. It ensures efficient resource utilisation and financial discipline.

Importance of budget

A budget is crucial in helping businesses and individuals to manage their finances efficiently by tracking revenues and expenses. The following are the importance of a budget:

Cash flow planning: A cash budget helps individuals or businesses to predict cash flows (inflows and outflows) to ensure liquidity.

Planning for money: A budget helps businesses and individuals to understand how much money they will receive from different sources and how much they will spend to operate a business in the future. This enables them to plan for future expenditure such as salaries, electricity bills, and rent. Therefore, without a budget, unexpected expenses can cause financial problems.

Ensure efficiency in cash utilisation: A budget ensures that money is used for essential expenditure, in accordance with the plan. It helps business owners to focus on what is necessary, such as purchasing stock, paying employees, or investing in business growth. Without

proper budgeting, money may be spent on unnecessary things, leading to shortages.

Making good decisions: When a business knows how much money it expects to have in a certain period, it can make better choices about spending and saving. A budget helps to decide whether to expand the business, buy new assets, or reduce expenditures. This avoids overspending and ensures that money is available when needed.

Controlling costs: A budget enables businesses and individuals to track spending and avoid unnecessary costs. Business owners can identify areas of overspending and take steps to control it. This practice helps to save money and enhances financial stability.

Monitoring performance: Businesses can compare their actual revenue and expenditure with the budget to check if they are on the right track. When actual spending exceeds the planned amount, they can identify ways to cut costs and stay within budget. This helps businesses to make improvements and work towards their financial goals.

Avoiding money shortages: A budget helps businesses to ensure they have enough money to pay for various business expenses, such as transport charges, salaries and wages and supplies. Without a budget, businesses and individuals can have difficulties of paying their bills, leading to financial problems.
